# Portfolio benchmark

Sample, not a ranking. I read five established engineering portfolios on 2026-10-20 and compared them to a feature branch preview at http://localhost:3001.
That preview does not mirror https://www.rijan.sh. Main has not been promoted, so production behavior is unverified. Everything below about our site is preview plus source, not shipped.
I ran no Lighthouse or screen reader audits, so I claim no scores. Sentences marked subjective are my judgment. The rest describe what I saw or read in source.

## Keep

Pinned commit sources and Limits sections on project pages. They make claims checkable.
Visible TODO(metric) placeholders instead of invented numbers. This honesty is required, keep it.
RSS, prev and next links, and table of contents on articles. The structure already anticipates a real archive.
No-JS readability and no homepage 3D. Keep both.
The paper and grid system with pure black dark. It reads as ours.
Exact resume href '/My Resume - Main.pdf' and the native form disclosure. Both are required, keep both.

## Changes, ranked by impact over cost

1. Give the hero one lane. Name the role you want next in plain words and cut the other two to a subordinate clause. Cost is an hour of rewriting.
2. Write one deep article with a table, dated updates, and a named baseline, modeled on https://arpan.sh/writing/skeladb-branch-optimization. The Rust and WASM boundary post is the candidate. Only you can supply the original work, the baseline choice, and the real numbers. Cost is a weekend, impact is large.
3. Put one measured number on each project card, even a modest one like suite size or benchmark input. If no number exists, say what was verified instead. Only you and past collaborators can supply counts, so ask them before publishing. Never estimate upward. Cost is small, impact is hiring legibility.
4. Tighten body line height from 1.8 toward 1.6 and give project summaries a shorter lede sentence. Cost is trivial.
5. Date the writing cards and show updated stamps when posts change, as https://arpan.sh/writing/skeladb-branch-optimization does. Undated technical claims decay fast, subjective.
6. Collapse contact to email plus resume, with the form kept behind the disclosure it already has. Do not remove the required href or disclosure. Cost is small.
7. Keep the custom 404 and production browser checks in CI. This implementation closes the missing recovery-page gap.
8. Fill each TODO(metric) only from records you actually hold. No fabricated benchmarks, no invented employers, no growth claims without a baseline and a date.

## Over and under

Over. The contact section offers four equivalent paths. The theme and token work is already good enough, and further polish there has falling returns. The dungeon experience stays off the homepage, which is correct.
Under. Long form writing with real numbers. Most of the sample lets long essays carry the argument for depth, and only you can provide original deep essays with measured outcomes. One essay with a comparison table would move the writing read more than any visual change.
